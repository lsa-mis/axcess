How a login scan works

You sign in yourself in a visible browser. Your password is typed into the site, never into Axcess.

Axcess
1
You
Choose a login scan
In New scan, choose “Site with a login or 2FA” and enter the “Page to scan after you sign in” (HTTPS).
2
You
Axcess
Open the sign-in browser
Select “Open browser to sign in”. Axcess opens a visible Chromium window with a fresh temporary profile.
3
You
Sign in yourself
Sign in directly with the site, including SSO and any two-factor step. Axcess never asks for or stores your password or codes.
4
You
Start the scan
Select “I’m signed in, start scan”.
Then Axcess takes over
5
Axcess
The session moves in memory
Axcess copies the session (cookies and site storage) in memory to a hidden scanning browser and closes the sign-in window. With “Show the scanning browser window” on, it keeps scanning in the visible window.
6
Axcess
Scan from where you landed
The scan crawls from where you landed and stays in scope. Rendered pages and screenshots are saved to the local report unless you choose “Don’t store rendered pages”.
7
Axcess
The session is destroyed
When the scan ends, the session is closed and the temporary profile is deleted. Nothing reusable is saved.
Login scans need an HTTPS site whose address resolves to a public IP address.
