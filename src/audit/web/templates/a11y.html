What this view shows. Axe-core auto-detects roughly 30-40% of WCAG 2.x AA success criteria — the ones that can be checked from the rendered DOM alone (color contrast, missing labels, ARIA misuse, focus order). The other ~60% require human judgment (whether alt text is meaningful, whether a heading is descriptive, whether a label is clear) and are not represented here. A clean axe pass is necessary, not sufficient, for WCAG conformance.
