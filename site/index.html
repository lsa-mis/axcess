Local-first · WCAG 2.2 A and AA · Free and open source

Test the pages behind the sign-in, and keep the evidence at home.

Axcess is a free accessibility scanner that runs on your computer. You sign in to the site yourself, including single sign-on and two-factor steps, and Axcess scans from there. The pages, screenshots, and findings stay on your machine.

1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) Level AA Barrier

Body text is too light to read against its background

What
Grey paragraph text on 14 pages measures 3.1:1 against white. The minimum is 4.5:1.
Why it matters
People with low vision, and anyone reading on a phone in sunlight, may not be able to read it.
Expected fix
Darken the text colour in the article stylesheet. One change fixes all 14 pages.

The problem

The pages that matter most are often the hardest to test

Course sites, application portals, and department tools often sit behind a university sign-in with two-factor authentication. Much of their content only appears after someone opens a menu, a tab, or a dialog. Hosted crawlers usually need extra setup to sign in, and content behind a click is often missed by rule-based checkers.

  1. Sign in yourself

    Axcess opens a normal browser window. You sign in directly with the site, and Axcess never sees your password or code.

  2. Axcess scans from there

    It visits the pages in the scope you set, opens menus, tabs, and dialogs, and runs its checks on every page it reaches.

  3. You get clear, local evidence

    Each issue shows what was found and exactly where, with fix guidance and rule documentation where they exist. It all stays on your computer.

What makes it different

Built for the gaps other tools find hard to reach

Scans behind a sign-in

Single sign-on and two-factor steps work because you complete them yourself. The session lives in memory, and no reusable login is saved.

Opens what visitors open

Axcess clicks through menus, tabs, and dialogs, tests what appears, and tells you which button revealed each problem.

Checks rule-based tools rarely automate

It measures reflow at phone width, text cut off at 200% zoom or with wider text spacing, and keyboard traps, and it finds text inside images.

Honest about certainty

Every result is a Barrier, Needs review, or Informational, so you know what to fix now and what a person should confirm first. What the groups mean.

Your evidence stays local

Reports, screenshots, and decisions live on your computer. There is no account, no telemetry, and no upload.

Works alongside your other tools

We use and like Siteimprove and axe DevTools. Axcess covers what is hard for them to reach. See the side-by-side comparison.

The workbench

An evidence workbench, not a scorecard

One score hides the details that make accessibility work actionable. Axcess keeps the page, the element, the screenshot, the rule, and the level of certainty together, so anyone can check a result for themselves.

Who it's for

Made for the people who do the work

Accessibility analysts

Triage findings with evidence, pair them with manual testing, and verify fixes on a rescan.

Editors and developers

See what to change, where it is, and how you will know it is done.

Leadership

See how thoroughly a site was checked and what to fix first, without a misleading score.

IT and security

Review a tool with no cloud dependency, no telemetry, and a documented data boundary.

What Axcess is not. Axcess produces accessibility evidence for a qualified person to review. Automated and AI-assisted results do not prove WCAG conformance, legal compliance, or the accessibility of an entire website, and they do not replace testing with people who use assistive technology.
