Body text is too light to read against its background

What
Grey paragraph text on 14 pages measures 3.1:1 against white. The minimum is 4.5:1.
Why it matters
People with low vision, and anyone reading on a phone in sunlight, may not be able to read it.
Expected fix
Darken the text colour in the article stylesheet. One change fixes all 14 pages.

In one minute

Scan. Review. Fix and verify.

Axcess is organised around the way an accessibility professional actually works, from the first web address to the follow-up scan that proves a fix landed.

  1. Scan a site

    Paste a web address, set how many pages to visit, and watch the scan run in a real browser. Sign in yourself if the site is behind a login.

  2. Review clear issues

    Every issue answers four questions: what is wrong, why it matters, what the fix looks like, and exactly where it is. Repeated problems are grouped so you fix the cause once.

  3. Hand off and verify

    Export an Excel workbook, a stakeholder report, or Jira tickets. Rescan later to see what is new, what is resolved, and what is still open.

The workbench

An evidence workbench, not a scorecard

One score hides the details that make accessibility work actionable. Axcess keeps the page, the element, the screenshot, the rule, and the confidence level connected so anyone can check a claim for themselves.

The dashboard of the desktop app. Recent scan targets are blurred in this preview.

Why teams trust it

Honest by design

Accessibility tools are easy to over-trust. Axcess is built to tell you what it saw, how sure it is, and what it could not check.

AAAThe level Axcess holds its own interface to. Every screen is tested for keyboard, screen reader, zoom, and contrast.

Evidence you can inspect

Every finding links back to the page, the element, a snippet, and often a screenshot, stored locally with the scan. Nothing is a bare number.

Clear about certainty

A deterministic rule failure, a behaviour the browser observed, and an AI suggestion are never mixed. Each result says which method produced it and whether a person still needs to confirm it.

Private by default

Scans, screenshots, and reports live in a local database on your computer. Sites behind a login are scanned after you sign in yourself; Axcess never sees your password.

Built for real, modern websites

Pages are rendered in a real browser, so single-page apps work. Axcess can also open menus, tabs, and dialogs to test the states a plain page load never shows.

What you get

Reports people can actually use

The same local evidence becomes whatever your audience needs, from a triage table for you to a ticket for a developer to a summary for a director.

Issue table

One scan-scoped table that groups repeated problems and states both numbers plainly, for example 19 issue groups across 965 occurrences.

Excel workbook

The hand-off file: Summary, Issues, Page Hotspots, Who's Affected, Coverage, Test Tracking, and Manual Evidence sheets with clickable links.

Stakeholder report

Scope, methods, limitations, results, recommended actions, and verification steps, written for readers who will never open the tool.

Tickets and data

Jira-ready CSV, plain CSV, and JSON so findings drop straight into the tools your team already uses.

Rescan comparison

Scan the same scope again and see what is new, what is resolved, and what is still open, instead of starting from zero.

Draft protection

A report exported before expert review is complete is clearly labelled DRAFT, so an unfinished list is never mistaken for a final audit.

Who it's for

Made for the people who do the work

Accessibility leads

Triage hundreds of findings in one sitting, defend every verdict, and verify fixes on rescan.

Editors and developers

Get a ticket that says what to change, where, and how you'll know it's done.

Leadership

See how thoroughly a site was evaluated and what to fix first, without a misleading score.

IT and security

Review a tool with no cloud dependency, no telemetry, and a documented data boundary.

What Axcess is not. Axcess produces accessibility evidence for a qualified person to review. Automated and AI-assisted results do not prove WCAG conformance, legal compliance, or the accessibility of an entire website, and they do not replace testing with people who use assistive technology.
